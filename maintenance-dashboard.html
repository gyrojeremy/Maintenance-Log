How these numbers are worked out

Breakdowns are entries logged as Reactive R&M or Temporary Repair.

MTTR (mean time to repair) is the average time on job for those breakdowns, as entered by the tech.

MTBF (mean time between failures) is the average number of days between one breakdown and the next at the same Shed, Area and Sub category. Locations with only one breakdown in the date range aren't included.

Time on job is hands-on time entered by the tech, not line downtime.
